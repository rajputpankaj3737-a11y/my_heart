A Journey Through Uncertainty

An emotional exploration of self-doubt, love, and the struggle to find one's path. Written on 05-01-2026 from a place of deep introspection.

Begin the Journey

The Weight of Existence

I'm really an unfortunate guy. Nothing in my life is going smoothly. I hate myself. I hate what I'm doing, why does it only happen to me? I don't know if it happens to me only or to others as well.

I am now 19 years old but soon I will be 20. Although I am currently studying, I'm always curious about my future. At my age, some people fall in love or it could be said that they are attracted to the opposite gender.

I always thought that I would never fall in love with anyone. It's a pity. When I was 14 years old, I was attracted to a girl for the first time. But with the passing of time, I don't know how I fell for her.

A Damaged Connection

Until now we are in a relationship but that's not always good. I always make her unhappy. I don't want to, but somehow I make her unhappy. I thought that I'm not worthy of her. I'm not worthy of her love.

I want to say that to her but I can't tell her. I want to part ways with her but I can't. It's not that I'm with someone else or I want to cheat on her. It's that I'm not worthy of her.

She is the best girl I've ever seen. I respect her. But I'm a guy who is not good at talking, not good looking, and in serious situations I always lose my confidence.

The Path Ahead

When I'm trying to say what is in my heart, I can't say it to her. I forget everything when I talk to her. At that moment my mind is like an empty vessel.

I want to talk with her but I have no words for her. Although I'm still focused on my career, I always miss her. Now our relationship is on a damaged bridge.

I am now on a path where I can't go back and can't go forward, and also if I don't move I know I will perish here.

Still I want to move forward, but I think if I go back or I stop here then it will be good for her. I don't know if I'm wrong or not.

But I wish for her to live happily in the future. I don't know when it will happen but her smile is a lot to me now.
